# Retrospective claim boundary for the v49 benchmark

This file is a publication-claim guardrail. It does not change the scientific
protocol or any locked scientific output.

## Study characterization

Paper13 should be described as a **retrospective benchmark audit**. It should
not be described as a preregistered confirmatory evaluation. Earlier
development iterations had already inspected final-test behavior before v49
designated the cost-only selector comparison as primary and demoted the legacy
`0.35` hard-risk contract to a stress test.

The following statement remains valid and important:

> Within the final locked v49 implementation, test labels are not used for
> model fitting or policy-parameter selection.

That statement must not be expanded into claims that the test set was
historically untouched, evaluated only once, or unavailable when the primary
protocol was chosen.

## Evaluation scope

- DOI grouping establishes **publication-source disjointness** for the active
  activation evaluation.
- It does not establish chemical-family, topology, linker-family, metal-node or
  structural-similarity independence.
- The final activation model is weak on the publication-source-disjoint test
  set and does not clearly exceed the training-fitted prior dummy baseline.
- The fixed-C/source-universe analysis is post-lock diagnostic evidence. It may
  demonstrate sensitivity to publication-source overlap but must not be framed
  as a causal decomposition.

## Benchmark-label fidelity boundary

- The binary solvent-removal outcomes are inherited from the MOFSimplify
  literature-extraction benchmark. They are **NLP-assigned benchmark
  annotations**, not candidate-level independently revalidated experimental
  ground truth generated by Paper13.
- The original MOFSimplify Technical Validation manually reviewed a random
  100-MOF subset: 78 NLP-assigned labels were clearly correct, two assignments
  were incorrect, and 20 extracted-text cases did not support a definitive
  solvent-removal-stability judgment (Nandy et al., *Scientific Data* 9, 74,
  2022; DOI: 10.1038/s41597-022-01181-0).
- Those subset-level counts establish a label-fidelity limitation but do not
  justify extrapolating a fixed mislabeling or ambiguity rate to every label in
  the Paper13 benchmark.
- Reader-facing counts should therefore be described as **benchmark-positive
  solvent-removal labels**, not experimentally validated successes, confirmed
  stable materials, or candidate-level ground truth.

## Selector scope

- The v49 cost-only selector table is a **retrospective descriptive comparison**.
- PortfolioBatch is an **illustrative deterministic policy** that exposes the
  trace/replay layer; v49 does not support a PortfolioBatch-superiority claim.
- Random empirical-tail frequencies and paired pool-resampling summaries are
  descriptive diagnostics, not p-values and not protocol-selection criteria.
  Canonical post-lock summaries are archived in
  `docs/descriptive_selector_diagnostics_v49R1.md` and `.csv`.

## Reader-facing terminology

- Machine field `hits` -> **benchmark-positive labels**.
- `proxy_total_cost` / cost budget -> **descriptor-complexity proxy cost** /
  **proxy budget**.
- `risk_score` -> **uncalibrated benchmark risk proxy**, never a failure
  probability.
- Avoid language such as "cost-efficient experimental discovery" unless the
  proxy nature is made explicit in the same sentence.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim:

- preregistered or untouched-test confirmation;
- test set used only once in the research history;
- chemical OOD validation;
- complete elimination of all leakage;
- PortfolioBatch superiority;
- measured synthesis-cost optimization;
- calibrated failure-risk optimization;
- prospective wet-lab validation of selected candidates.
